Open the Vonage Settings card you just added. Setup

Set up your webhooks

Webhooks are what make inbound work - they let Vonage deliver incoming WhatsApp, RCS and SMS into your inbox. You point your Vonage application at two URLs, once.

Your customerWhatsApp / RCS / SMS

HubSpotInbox + timeline

These are wired automatically when you save your Vonage account in step 2 of Setup. If you ever need to set them by hand (Vonage dashboard → Applications → your app → Edit → Messages capability):

https://YOUR-BACKEND-HOST/2v/webhooks/inbound?portal=YOUR_PORTAL_ID
https://YOUR-BACKEND-HOST/2v/webhooks/status
  1. Open dashboard.vonage.com → Applications and pick the app you connected in Setup.
  2. Click Edit, and under Capabilities enable Messages (and Voice for the softphone).
  3. Paste the Inbound URL (with your portal id) and Status URL above, then Save changes.
  4. Under Linked numbers, link the WhatsApp / SMS numbers (and RCS agent) you want to use.
The ?portal= tag routes inbound messages to your HubSpot account only. Every account on this integration sends and receives exclusively through its own Vonage application and numbers.
Using it · 3

Automate with HubSpot Workflows

The integration adds three native actions to the workflow builder, so messages can be sent automatically - no Operations Hub, webhooks or custom code needed. Any contact-based workflow can message people the moment they enrol.

  1. Go to Automation → Workflows and create (or edit) a contact-based workflow.
  2. Click + to add an action and search for “Vonage”.
  3. Pick an action, fill in the fields, turn the workflow on.

Good to know

Everything is logged

Every message sent and received is written to the contact's timeline as an activity, so the whole team sees the full history without leaving HubSpot. The inbox keeps the live thread; the timeline keeps the record.
