1 - Before you send anything

Three things that explain almost every problem

WhatsApp is not SMS. Meta (the company behind WhatsApp) decides what a business may send and when. Vonage carries the message; HubSpot is where you work. Once these three rules are clear, the rest is easy.

1. The 24-hour window

When a customer writes to you, a 24-hour conversation window opens. Inside it you can send free text, images, anything. When it closes - or if the customer never wrote first - you can only send an approved template.

2. Templates are approved by Meta

Every template is reviewed by WhatsApp before it can be used, per language. Vonage submits it for you and shows you the status; neither Vonage nor the integration can skip or speed up the review.

3. The customer must have opted in

You may only message people who agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from you (a tick box, a form, a reply). Messaging people who did not opt in gets your number's quality rating lowered and, eventually, blocked.

Who does what

PartyRoleWhat you see from them
WhatsApp / MetaOwns the rules. Approves or rejects templates, delivers to the handset, rates your number's quality.Template status (Approved / Rejected / Paused), delivered and read ticks, the customer's replies.
VonageYour WhatsApp number and Business Account live here. Carries every message in and out, holds the templates, bills per conversation.The Vonage dashboard (logs, balance), template submission results, error codes.
HubSpot + the Vonage integrationWhere you work: contact cards, Inbox, Templates card, workflow actions. Talks to Vonage using your Vonage account.The Vonage cards on contacts, the Settings page, workflow action results.
The sentence to remember: "If the customer did not write to me in the last 24 hours, I need an approved template - sent exactly the way it was approved."

What a WhatsApp message costs

WhatsApp bills per conversation (a 24-hour window), not per message, and the price depends on who opened it and the category: a marketing template opens the most expensive conversation, a utility template a cheaper one, and a reply inside a window the customer opened is cheaper still. Vonage passes these charges through on your Vonage account. This is why the right category matters, and why a message that is rejected costs nothing.

2 - Templates

What a template is made of

A template is a pre-written message that WhatsApp has read and approved. You can only change the parts you declared as variables. Everything else must go out word for word.

Lesson cancelled
Hi {{1}}, your lesson with {{2}} on {{3}} has been cancelled. Reply to this message to rebook, or open your schedule below.
Acme - reply STOP to opt out
Open my schedule

An example with a header, three body variables, a footer and one button.

Name
lesson_cancelled - lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only. Choose a name that says what it is; you cannot rename it later.
Language
One template per language. en, en_US, pl and en_GB are four different templates, each approved separately.
Header
Optional. Short text (may hold one variable), or an image / document / video.
Body
Required. The message. Variables are written {{1}}, {{2}}, {{3}} in order, never skipping a number.
Footer
Optional small print, e.g. how to opt out. No variables.
Buttons
Optional: quick replies, a website link, a phone number, or a copy-code button.

Variables: the number one cause of "sent but never arrived"

When you send a template you must supply exactly as many values as the template has variables - no more, no fewer. A template with no variables must be sent with no values at all. WhatsApp first accepts the request and only afterwards rejects it (error 1022, "invalid template or template parameters"), which is why a wrong send can look successful for a moment.

Right
Template body: "Hi {{1}}, your lesson on {{2}} is confirmed."
Values sent: Anna, Monday 10:00 - two variables, two values.
Wrong
Template body: "Hi, your lesson has been cancelled." (no variables)
Values sent: Anna - one value for zero variables. Rejected after "sent".
Tip: in HubSpot the Templates card shows each template's text. Count the {{n}} markers before you fill in any variable field. The workflow action now checks this for you and refuses a mismatch with a clear message, but a quick look saves a round trip.

3 - Categories, languages, variables

Pick the right category - Meta will check

The category you choose must match what the message actually says. Meta reads the text and will reject or re-categorise a template whose words do not fit, which changes what you pay.

CategoryUse it forExample that passesWill not pass as this category
Utility
cheapest		A specific transaction or account update the customer expects: confirmations, changes, reminders, receipts, delivery updates."Your lesson on {{1}} has been moved to {{2}}. Reply to confirm."Anything with an offer, a discount, "don't miss", a promotion - even one sentence of it.
MarketingPromotions, offers, newsletters, re-engagement, anything that invites a purchase or a visit. Also any message that mixes an update with a promotion."New autumn courses are open - book by Friday and get 10% off."Nothing is refused for being marketing; but it costs more and needs clear opt-in.
AuthenticationOne-time passcodes only, using Meta's fixed wording with a copy-code button."{{1}} is your verification code."Any extra text, links or branding - the wording is fixed by Meta.

Languages

  • Submit the template in the language the text is actually written in. A Polish text submitted as en_US may be approved but it is confusing later: when sending you must pass the exact code it was approved in (en_US is not en).
  • Need the same message in two languages? Submit the same name twice, once per language. The integration sends the one matching the language you enter.
  • The Templates card in HubSpot shows the language next to each template - copy it from there.

Writing variables that get approved

  • Number them in order: {{1}}, {{2}}, {{3}}. Not {{1}} then {{3}}.
  • Never start or end the body with a variable, and never put two variables next to each other with nothing between them - both are automatic rejections.
  • Give Meta realistic sample values when submitting (the integration asks for them). "Anna", "Monday 10:00" - not "test", "xxx" or "123".
  • Keep variables for things that genuinely change (a name, a date, an order number). A template that is only variables ("{{1}} {{2}} {{3}}") is rejected as a blank cheque.
  • Links should be in a button, or a full fixed URL in the text. A variable that is a whole URL is a common rejection reason.

4 - Approval

Getting a template approved first time

Most templates are reviewed automatically within minutes; some take up to 24 hours. A rejection costs you a day, so it is worth getting the submission right.

Before you press Submit

  1. Read it as the customer. Would you understand who it is from and why you got it, with no other context? Add your brand name if not.
  2. Check the category honestly. One promotional sentence makes the whole template Marketing.
  3. Check spelling and grammar. Meta rejects templates with obvious errors or odd formatting (ALL CAPS, lots of !!!, emoji walls).
  4. Check the variables. In order, not first or last, not adjacent, with realistic samples.
  5. Marketing? Add a way out. A footer like "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" is expected for marketing templates and improves approval odds and quality rating.
  6. Submit once. Then wait. Submitting the same name again while the first is under review produces an "already exists" error on the card - it does not speed anything up.

What the statuses mean

PendingUnder review. Usually minutes, up to 24 h. Do nothing.
ApprovedReady to send, in that language only.
RejectedMeta gives a reason (shown on the card). Fix the text and submit under a new name or edit and resubmit - do not just resend the same text.
PausedMeta paused it because customers blocked or reported it. Stop using it; after the pause ends, rewrite before reuse.
DisabledPaused three times, or a policy breach. Cannot be used again - create a new, different one.
ErrorThe integration could not submit (duplicate name, bad variable, missing sample). The card shows the exact message.

Common rejection reasons

  • Promotional words in a Utility template
  • Variables at the start/end, adjacent, or out of order
  • Placeholder samples like "test" or "{{1}}" left in
  • Links to shortened URLs (bit.ly etc.) or to a different brand's domain
  • Text that could be abusive, misleading, or asks for sensitive data (passwords, card numbers)
  • Too vague: "Hello, click here"
Quality rating: WhatsApp scores your number Green / Yellow / Red from customer reactions in the last 7 days (blocks, reports, "not interested"). Low quality lowers your daily sending limit and pauses templates. Only message people who opted in, make opting out easy, and do not re-send the same marketing template to the same people repeatedly.

5 - In HubSpot

Using the Vonage integration day to day

Everything runs on your own Vonage account and WhatsApp number. Set up once by an admin; after that the cards on each contact do the work.

One-time setup (admin)

Marketplace › Connected apps › Vonage Messaging › Settings. Enter the Vonage API key and secret, the Application ID and private key, and the WhatsApp number. The WhatsApp Business Account is detected automatically from the number. Save & verify. Until this is done every card says "not configured".

Templates card

Lists every template on your WhatsApp Business Account with its language and live status. From here you can submit a new template (name, language, category, text with variables and sample values, optional header image), send an approved one to the contact you are on, and delete one from the account (permanent, every language).

Send card (New message)

Free text, images and files to the contact. Works only inside an open 24-hour window - if the customer has not written recently, use a template instead. Choose the channel (WhatsApp, SMS, RCS) at the top.

Inbox card

Replies arrive here and on the contact timeline. A reply from the customer opens a new 24-hour window, so you can answer with free text. Watch for "STOP" and honour it.

Timeline notes

Every send - manual or from a workflow - is logged as a note on the contact ("[WHATSAPP sent] template ..."). Use it to see what a contact already received before sending again.

Sending a template to one contact

  1. Open the contact and its Templates card. Only Approved templates can be sent.
  2. Pick the template and language. Read its text; count the {{n}} markers.
  3. Fill exactly that many values, in order. None at all if the template has none.
  4. Send. Then check the contact's timeline a minute later - "delivered" or "read" means it arrived; a rejection shows the reason.
Phone numbers: the contact needs a mobile number in international format in the Mobile phone number field (the integration tries Mobile first, then Phone). +48 533 507 739 is fine; a landline or a number that is not on WhatsApp is not, and the message will not arrive.

8 - Calls, SMS and RCS

The other channels, briefly

Calling

  1. 1Two ways to call: the Call button on the Messaging card dials the contact's number immediately; the Dialer view (Templates card › view switch › Dialer) lets you type any number.
  2. 2What the customer sees: the Voice number from Settings as caller ID. Choose a number in the customer's country where possible.
  3. 3Where you talk: the call is placed through Vonage and connected to your configured phone/softphone; HubSpot logs "[CALL] placed" on the contact timeline.
  4. 4Before using it: the Voice number must be linked to the Vonage application, and your Vonage account must have voice enabled for the destination country.

SMS

When

Short transactional notices, people without WhatsApp, fallback when a WhatsApp number is not reachable. No 24-hour window, no templates to approve - but sender rules apply per country (some countries require a registered sender ID or a local number).

Watch out

160 characters per segment (70 with Polish or other accented characters) - long texts cost several segments. Links shorten the message but can hurt deliverability on some networks. Keep an opt-out line for marketing.

RCS

When

Rich messages (images, cards, buttons, suggested replies) in the phone's native Messages app on Android. Needs an approved RCS agent (the "RCS sender ID" in Settings). Not every handset or carrier supports it yet.

Fallback

If the phone cannot receive RCS the message is not delivered as RCS - decide in your workflow what happens next (usually an SMS branch). Templates for RCS are built in the same New / edit view with channel = RCS.

Rule of thumb: WhatsApp for conversations and anything the customer should reply to; SMS for short one-way notices and as the universal fallback; RCS where you want rich content on Android and have an agent approved. The workflow actions exist for all three: "Send WhatsApp template via Vonage", "Send SMS via Vonage", "Send RCS via Vonage".

9 - Automation

Workflow actions: sending automatically

In any contact-based workflow you can add the action "Send WhatsApp template via Vonage" (and "Send SMS via Vonage" / "Send RCS via Vonage"). Because nobody is watching when it fires, the fields must be exactly right.

The fields

Send WhatsApp template via Vonage
zmiana_godziny_lekcji
Exactly as on the Templates card - lowercase, underscores.
en_US
The code it was approved in, from the Templates card.
leave empty - this template has no variables
SaveCancel

The action panel as it appears in the workflow editor (illustration). HubSpot tokens such as the contact's first name can be used as variable values when the template has placeholders.

What the action reports back

sentVonage accepted it. Delivery is confirmed on the timeline a moment later.
template_variable_mismatchYou filled too many or too few variables. The message says how many the template expects.
template_not_approvedThat name/language is Pending, Rejected or Paused.
no_phone_on_contactThe contact has no Mobile/Phone value.
vonage_not_configuredSettings page not completed for this portal.
vonage_4xx ...Vonage refused it; the detail says why (wrong sender, number format...).

Before switching a workflow on

  1. Test on yourself. Enrol one contact - your own - and wait for the WhatsApp to arrive before enrolling anyone else.
  2. Check the enrolment trigger. A workflow that re-enrols people every time a property changes will message them every time. Add "re-enrolment off" or a suppression list.
  3. Respect opt-in. Add a filter: only contacts with WhatsApp consent = yes. For marketing templates this is not optional.
  4. Mind the window. Automated sends are almost always outside the 24-hour window, so they must be templates - the action only sends templates for this reason.
  5. One template per branch. Do not try to make one template do five jobs with variables; Meta rejects "blank cheque" templates and customers find them odd.
What went wrong in the real case: the template "zmiana_godziny_lekcji" has no variables, but the action had a value in Variable {{1}}. HubSpot said "sent" because Vonage accepted the request; WhatsApp then rejected it (error 1022) and nothing arrived. The action now catches this before sending and shows template_variable_mismatch instead.

10 - Do and don't

The short list

Templates
DoWrite the template as a complete, polite message with your brand name in it. Use realistic sample values. Number variables in order.
Don'tStart or end with a variable, put variables next to each other, use "test"/"xxx" samples, or mix an offer into a Utility template.
DoSubmit once per language and wait for the status. Note the exact language code.
Don'tClick Submit repeatedly, or submit a Polish text as en_US "because it was the default".
Sending
DoSend a template exactly as approved: same name, same language code, exactly as many variables as it has.
Don'tFill a variable field "just in case", or send free text to someone who has not written to you in 24 hours.
DoCheck the contact timeline after the first send of any new template - look for delivered/read.
Don'tAssume "sent" means received. "Sent" means Vonage accepted it; WhatsApp confirms delivery a moment later.
Audience
DoMessage only contacts who opted in to WhatsApp, and honour STOP immediately (the integration records inbound replies; add the contact to a suppression list).
Don'tUpload a purchased list, re-send the same marketing template to the same people within days, or message at night in the customer's time zone.
DoKeep the Mobile phone field in international format (+48..., +44...).
Don'tRely on the Phone field holding a landline - it will never receive WhatsApp.
Account
DoKeep the Vonage balance topped up or on auto-reload; keep the display name and profile picture of your WhatsApp number current.
Don'tDelete a template that a live workflow still uses, or change the WhatsApp number in Settings without telling whoever owns the workflows.
